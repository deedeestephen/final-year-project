## Interview (after the SUS)

1. What did you like most? What was hardest?
2. Was anything confusing or worrying? Where?
3. The assistant: would you ask it questions about your health? Why, or why not? Did you trust its answers?
4. Reading aloud and speaking to the phone: were they useful to you? Was the voice clear?
5. In which language would you most like to use the app?
6. Would you recommend it to a friend or relative who is going for screening?
