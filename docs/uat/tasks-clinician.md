Note: the prototype's AI uses practice (mock) models, so this checks the report's design and labels, not the AI's accuracy.

## Interview (after the SUS)

1. Which tasks would you do every day? Which were slow or awkward?
2. Working without internet: was it clear what was saved and what was sent?
3. The AI report: what would you need to see before you would use such a report in practice?
4. Is anything missing that you would need in your clinic?
5. Would the assistant be useful for your patients? For you?
