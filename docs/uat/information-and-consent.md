## Information sheet

**Study title:** Testing a mobile health app for prostate cancer screening in Zambia (a research prototype)

**Researcher:** [Your name], final-year student, Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, ZCAS University. Supervisor: [supervisor's name]. Contact: [phone or e-mail].

**Ethics approval:** ZCAS University Ethics Review Board, reference [number]; National Health Research Authority, reference [number].

**What is this study about?**
I have built a test version of a phone app and a website. They help clinics record prostate cancer screening, help pathologists review slides, and give men general information about the prostate, including an assistant that answers questions and can read text aloud. I want to find out whether the app is easy to use, and what needs to change.

**What will I be asked to do?**
You will use the app on a phone we give you, for about 45 minutes. You will be asked to do some everyday tasks, such as reading an article or recording a made-up screening, and to say aloud what you are thinking while you do them. Then you answer ten short questions about how easy it was, and a few questions about your experience.

**Important:**
- Everything in the app is **made up (synthetic)**. Please do not type your own name, phone number, NRC number or any real patient's details.
- The app is a **test version**. Its "AI results" are practice output, not real results, and nothing in it is about your health.
- The assistant gives **general information only**. It is not a doctor. If you have a health concern, please speak to a clinician.

**Do I have to take part?**
No. Taking part is your choice. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and it will not affect your care or your work in any way.

**What will happen to the information?**
We write notes about how the tasks went and your answers to the questions, under a code, not your name. The list that links your name to your code is kept locked and separately, and is destroyed when the study ends [date]. If you agree, we may record your voice while you think aloud; the recording is used only to check our notes and is deleted at the end of the study. Results are reported without names, and quotes are given under codes only.

**Are there risks or benefits?**
There are no known risks beyond the time you give. There is no payment [or: you will receive ... for your time and transport]. Your feedback will help make the app easier to use.

**Questions or complaints:** the researcher [contact], the supervisor [contact], or the ethics committee [contact].
